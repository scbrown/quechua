Quechua vocabulary

A shared vocabulary for knowledge, governance, and code intelligence.

Namespace: https://scbrown.github.io/quechua/ns#. Prefix: quechua:.

Download the Turtle vocabulary. This catalog declares term identities. SHACL shapes that use them are published separately, in work-item shapes camayoc shapes and ontology shapes. Kept terms are aligned to public vocabularies in alignments.ttl; deprecated terms name the public term to use instead. Neither installs anything or migrates data. Instance identifiers remain under their original namespaces.
